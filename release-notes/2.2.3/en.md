# What's new in Wivo 2.2.3

## Two people, one job: nothing is overwritten

When you save a job, Wivo now saves only what you changed. A stage, a doer,
a note or a revision someone else saved while you had the job open stays as
they left it.

If you both changed the same thing, nothing is saved and Wivo shows
**Someone else changed this while you were editing**, with what you both
changed. **Reload** shows their version and keeps your other changes in the
form.

The same goes for prices and work types, pay rules and workflow templates.
A price saved without a connection is no longer lost: Wivo sends it again by
itself.

## Changes from another computer show at once

A note, a price or a stage changed on another computer now shows straight
away, and always when you open the job. Before, it could take until Wivo was
restarted. If a save or a stage move fails, Wivo says why.

## Duplicate makes a new job

**Duplicate** on a job now creates a new job. Before, saving the copy
changed the original job.
