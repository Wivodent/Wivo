# What's new in Wivo 2.1.0

## Settings, easier to find your way

Settings now opens on one page of large tiles: **Clinic**, **Jobs**, **Money**,
**Help** and **My settings**. Click a tile to open it, and **Settings** at the
top to go back. You only see what your clinic uses.

The owner also sees **Set up your clinic**: a short list of first steps, each
marked done or not, with a button that takes you to the right place. You can
hide it when you are done.

## Adding a job

- **Production:** each work item has its own card with its teeth and machine.
  The stages and the dates each have their own section.
- **Patient and order:** the patient and who ordered the work are now two
  separate boxes, and the notes have their own section.

Longer explanations are behind the round **i** buttons.

## Prices

Each work type is a larger card: the price for the client, the volume price
and our cost, with bigger price fields and a clear **Per job / Per tooth**
switch. Typing a cost name no longer jumps out of the field after each letter.

## Payroll

Open a person to see this month's amount, the unpaid lines and the button to
confirm the payout at the top. Rarely changed settings are under
**More settings**.

In Latvia, Wivo now estimates the payslip with Latvian rates: social tax,
income tax, the tax booklet, dependents and pensioners. Set them for each
person under **More settings**.

## Also

- Buttons for parts your clinic does not use are hidden.
- **Request a feature** in Settings sends Wivo an e-mail.
- "Unsorted" is now called **Scanner files**.
