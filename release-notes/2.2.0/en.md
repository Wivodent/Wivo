# What's new in Wivo 2.2.0

## Implants when you pick the teeth

In **Add job → Teeth**, each tooth you pick has a small **+** above it. Click it
to mark an implant, click again to take the mark off. Work built on implants,
such as an implant crown or an implant bridge, marks the teeth for you. Under
the chart you see how many teeth, implants and pontics there are.

## Who ordered the work

**Patient & order** now asks who ordered it: **Our clinic** (pick the doctor),
**Another clinic**, or a **Dentist / technician**, with their own order number.
Under **Clients**, mark each client as a clinic or as a dentist or technician.

## Options on a work type

A work type can come in variants, such as **Framework: Metal bar** or
**Zirconia framework**. Set them up on the work type's card in
**Settings → Prices**, each with the extra for the client and our cost. An
option can have its own price on each work type: a metal bar can cost more on
an All-on-6 than on an All-on-4. The **Option library** under the cards holds
the default prices.

When you add a job, pick the option with one click. Its price goes into the
job's price and the invoice, and its cost into the margin.

## Work type catalogue

**Settings → Prices → Add from catalogue** shows common lab work with
pictures. Click **Add** and it becomes one of your work types, in your clinic's
language, ready for your price. The pictures also show when you add a job.

## Who makes an option, and pay rules by option

On the job card, the job page and the phone, each chosen option has a person
button, like the stages: the job's technician makes it unless you pick someone
else, who is then told in the bell.

In **Payroll**, a pay rule can apply **Only with option**: for example 50 € more
for an All-on-4 with a metal bar. It pays whoever makes the option, and comes
before a rule for the work type alone.

## Latvia: employer's tax per person

Finance and Statistics now count the employer's social tax for each person:
20.77% for a pensioner, 23.59% for everyone else.
