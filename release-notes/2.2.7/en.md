# What's new in Wivo 2.2.7

## Jobs

- **Something new on a job?** A small mark on the job card shows notes,
  files, messages and stage moves by others that you have not seen yet.
  Opening the job clears it, on all your devices.
- **Skipped stages are hidden** on the job. The arrows on the board cards
  jump over them, and a job cannot be dragged into one.
- **Clearer messages** when a job cannot be moved. If the stage is switched
  off for that job, Wivo says so and offers **Turn on and move**.

## Team

- **The owner can change a worker's name** on the Team page. A worker
  without a name shows as "No name yet".
