# What's new in Wivo 2.2.2

## A work type's kind is now your own setting

In **Settings → Prices**, every work type's card has **Type**: Single tooth,
Bridge (2+ teeth), Full arch or Per job (no teeth). It decides which teeth a
new job asks for, not the type's name. Your work types keep the kind they
had until you change it.

Design work, such as a crown design or smile design, is no longer taken for a
bridge. If a bridge has only one tooth, the owner sees **Not a bridge? Change
type** beside the warning, which opens that work type's card. The new job is
kept as a draft.
