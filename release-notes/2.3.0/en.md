# What's new in Wivo 2.3.0

## Wivo Network: orders from outside

- **Outside technicians and clinics can send you orders.** They appear on
  the new **Orders** page, where you accept them, write to the client and
  mark the work as sent.
- **Invite a client** with your invite link (**Copy invite link** on the
  Orders page). Clients order from your catalogue: you choose which work
  types they can order and which price they see.
- **Each client can have their own prices** and a discount, on the
  client's card.

## Orders and jobs

- **Implants** can be marked on the order, and you can **sweep across the
  teeth** with the mouse or a finger to pick many at once. **Undo** takes
  back the whole sweep.
- **The order reference fills in by itself** (for example
  TF-DE-261127-001). You can switch this off in Settings.
- **Files in messages**, both ways. Ask the client to look at something
  with **Needs the client's review**; they answer Approve or Request
  changes.
- **Received and delivery problems:** the client confirms the work arrived,
  or reports a problem, which stays on the Orders page until you resolve it.
- **Feedback:** after the work is received, the doctor says **Fitted ✓** or
  **Needs adjustment**, and can rate the work from 1 to 5.
- **The conversation folds** so it takes less room; it opens by itself when
  there are unread messages.

## Invoices

- **Invoices in Latvian or English**, chosen per client, and **VAT exempt**
  invoices with the reason printed.
- **Send by e-mail** straight from the invoice, with the PDF attached.
- **Automatic invoicing:** an invoice is made when the client confirms the
  work, per job or once a month (Settings → Invoices, off by default).
- **Nothing disappears by mistake:** only a draft can be deleted. An issued
  invoice is cancelled or gets a credit note. A deleted job goes to
  **Deleted jobs**, where it can be restored.

## Also

- **A version line** beside "What's new" shows exactly which Wivo you are
  running.
