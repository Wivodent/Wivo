# What's new in Wivo 2.2.4

## Files and stage people show at once

A file added to a job, or a person put on a stage, on another computer now
shows straight away, without opening the job again.

## Saves that cannot be cut in half

A workflow template, a stage's default people and a job's notes are now
saved in one step. A lost connection leaves them as they were: a template no
longer ends up without its stages, and two notes added at the same moment
both stay.

If someone else changed the same online booking hours while you were
editing, Wivo says so instead of saving over them.
