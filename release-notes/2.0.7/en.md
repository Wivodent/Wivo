# What's new in Wivo 2.0.7

## A new licence arrives by itself

When your licence is renewed or changed, for example with a new module, more
users or a later date, Wivo now installs it by itself within about half an
hour. Nobody has to paste a key.

The owner sees **"Licence updated"** at the top of the window, with what
changed. **Settings → Licence** shows the licence in full.

Wivo checks that the licence is Wivo's and is meant for your clinic before it
installs it. Pasting a key in Settings → Licence still works as before.
