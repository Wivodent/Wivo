# What's new in Wivo 2.2.6

## Moving a job on

- **A job in a skipped stage** no longer loses its buttons. The job page says
  "This job is in a skipped stage" and offers **Move to next active stage**.
  Next and Previous go to the next and previous stages that are not skipped.
- **Skipping the stage a job is at** moves the job on to the next active stage.
- **On the phone**, a big **Move to next stage →** button sits under the
  stages, with a smaller **← Back**.
