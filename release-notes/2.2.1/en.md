# What's new in Wivo 2.2.1

## Choose a work type's picture

In **Settings → Prices**, click the picture on a work type's card, or
**Change picture**, and pick any 3D picture or line icon. Your choice shows
everywhere the work type appears, also when you add a job. **Automatic** puts
back the picture Wivo picks itself.

## Better automatic pictures

Wivo now goes by the work type's own name first, and a name of several words
wins over a single word. A surgical guide, smile design or crown design no
longer shows an implant crown or a bridge.
