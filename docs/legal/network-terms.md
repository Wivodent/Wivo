## 1. What Wivo Network is

Wivo Network lets dental technicians, doctors, clinics and labs ("users") find dental clinics and labs that use Wivo ("clinics"), ask to connect to them, and send them orders for dental work.

Wivo Network itself holds only your account, the directory of clinics and the state of your connections. **Your orders, files, messages, prices and ratings go directly to the clinic you send them to and are stored in that clinic's own database.** Wivo does not keep them in Wivo Network.

Wivo Network is a tool for professionals. It is not for patients, and it is not a medical device: it does not recommend or rank any treatment.
