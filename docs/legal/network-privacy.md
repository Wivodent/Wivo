This notice explains what personal data Wivo Network uses, why, and what rights you have. It is for people who use Wivo Network: dental technicians, doctors, clinics and labs, and the people who work for them.

## 1. Who is responsible

There are two kinds of data in Wivo Network, with different people responsible for them.

- **Your account and the directory.** [COMPANY NAME], registry code [REG CODE], [ADDRESS], [CONTACT EMAIL] ("Wivo", "we") is the controller of your Wivo Network account, your connections and the clinic directory.
- **Your orders.** Orders, files, messages, prices, ratings and notices are stored in the database of the clinic you send them to, not in Wivo Network. **That clinic is the controller** of what it receives. You are the controller of the data you decide to send, including any patient data in it.
- Where a clinic uses Wivo's software and services, Wivo may act as that clinic's processor under a data processing agreement. Wivo then follows only the clinic's instructions.

## 2. What we hold about you, and why

**Your account**

- Sign-in e-mail, password (stored only as a hash) and your second-factor setup. To let you sign in securely.
- Name, kind of account (person or company), role, company name, country and city. To show the clinics you connect to who you are.
- Whether clinics may e-mail you about your orders, and your billing e-mail if you give one. So clinics can send notices and invoices to the right address.
- The version of these terms you accepted and when. To show that you accepted them.
- Technical data kept by our hosting provider: sign-in times, sessions, IP address and browser. To keep the service secure and find misuse.

**Your connections**

- Which clinics you asked to connect to, whether the connection is waiting, confirmed or ended, and when. To let you order from those clinics.

**The directory**

- The name, country and city of clinics that take orders, and their address in Wivo. This is clinic data; it may include a person's name if the clinic is a sole trader.

We do not hold orders, files, messages, prices, ratings or patient data in Wivo Network.

## 3. Legal bases

- **Contract** (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)): your account, connections and passing your profile to the clinics you choose, so that we can provide Wivo Network as agreed in the Terms of Use.
- **Legitimate interest** (art. 6(1)(f)): security logs and preventing misuse.
- **Legal obligation** (art. 6(1)(c)): where the law requires us to keep or hand over data.
- **Consent** (art. 6(1)(a)): e-mail notices from clinics, which you can switch off in your profile at any time.

## 4. What a clinic receives when you connect

When you open a clinic in Wivo Network, your browser gets a signed pass from us that lasts 5 minutes and works only for that clinic. It carries your name, kind of account, role, company, country, sign-in e-mail, your e-mail choice and your billing e-mail if you gave one. Not your city.

The clinic keeps this in its own database, adds you to its client list when it accepts you, and keeps your orders, files, messages, notices and ratings there. From then on **the clinic is responsible for that data**, under its own privacy notice. Ask the clinic about it directly.

## 5. Patient data in orders

- An order has a field for your own patient reference only, never a name, personal code, birth date or contact details.
- Scans, photos, X-rays and other files may be health data (GDPR art. 9). They go from your browser straight to the clinic's database. Wivo Network does not store them.
- Before upload, Wivo removes hidden information from photos (location, camera, dates) and names written inside 3D scan files. It does not change X-ray (DICOM) files, which can contain the patient's name.
- You and the clinic are each responsible for having a lawful basis for patient data, and for informing the patient.

## 6. Who else processes the data

- **Supabase** (database, sign-in and server functions for Wivo Network), hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
- **Cloudflare** (delivers the Wivo Network web pages). It sees the requests your browser makes for the pages, including your IP address.
- **Supabase's e-mail service**, which sends the e-mail that confirms your address.
- **Each clinic you connect to**, which receives your profile (section 4).

E-mail notices and invoices about your orders are sent by the clinic, with its own e-mail providers.

## 8. How long we keep it

- Your account, profile and connections: while you have an account. When your account is deleted, they are deleted with it.
- Your accepted terms version: while you have an account, then [RETENTION PERIOD] for evidence.
- Security and sign-in logs at our hosting provider: as long as the provider's plan keeps them, [LOG RETENTION].
- What a clinic received from you stays with that clinic under its own rules, also after your account or the connection ends.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct it (most of it you can change yourself in your profile);
- delete your account and its data;
- restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- give you your data in a machine-readable form.

You can withdraw consent for e-mail notices at any time in your profile.

Write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We answer within one month. For orders, files and messages, ask the clinic you sent them to: it is the controller of that data.

You may also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, www.aki.ee) or to the data protection authority of your own country, for example the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija, www.dvi.gov.lv).

## 10. Security

Every sign-in needs a second factor. A user sees only their own account. A clinic's pass lasts 5 minutes. A file link lasts 60 seconds. The clinic's session for you ends after the time the clinic set (8 hours unless the clinic changed it) or when the tab is closed. Clinics store your sessions only as a one-way hash.

We may update this notice. We tell you about important changes in Wivo Network before they take effect.
